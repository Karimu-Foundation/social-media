## Education
- **School dropout rate** (newly adopted Theory of Change metric, measured across
  the 11 combined years of primary + secondary): Dabil **5.83%/yr** →
  Arri **3.34%/yr** → Ayalagaya **1.39%/yr**. `[NL Jul-26]`
  **This is the single strongest proof-of-model stat Karimu now has** — the number
  falls as a community moves through the trajectory.
- **Close to 90% of children reach high school — in Ayalagaya.** `[NL Jul-26]`

  outperform girls). `[SC]` `[Classy26]`

  enter high school; ranked third-best school in the country (award, 2022). `[SC]`

  High School. `[SC]`

  bathroom projects because of a government cost-sharing partnership. `[NL Jul-26]`

  2028, so Karimu expects a **surge in secondary enrolment** and is building
