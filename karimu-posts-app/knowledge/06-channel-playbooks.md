# 06 — Channel Playbooks

Never publish identical text across channels. Adapt hook, length, CTA and format.

---

## Instagram

> **The feed is video-first.** In the last 12 posts sampled (Aug–Sep 2026), **9 were
> video**. Many of the graphics in the grid are designed cards *animated and posted
> as Reels*, not static posts. When you finish a card, ask whether it should ship as
> a Reel. Format catalogue: `templates/post-formats.md`.

- **Primary persona:** Global Citizen, younger Compassionate Contributor, Adventure Philanthropist.
- **Job:** followers, saves, shares, DM/profile-link traffic to newsletter.
- **Formats:** single image 4:5 · carousel 4:5 (3–8 slides) · Reel 9:16, 15–60s · Stories 9:16.
- **Caption:** **60–90 words, hard ceiling 90.** Strong first line (only ~125 characters show before "more").
  Short lines with breaks. Up to 3 emoji.
- **Links do not work in captions.** Never write "click the link below."
  Say: **"Link in bio"** and make sure the bio link is the right destination.
- **Hashtags:** 8–15, in the first comment or at the end after a line break.
  Core set: `#Karimu #Tanzania #EndExtremePoverty #CommunityLed #NonProfit
  #ImpactStories #Manyara #GlobalDevelopment #WaterIsLife #GirlsEducation
  #SocialImpact #Asante`
  Add 2–4 theme-specific tags per post.
- **Alt text required.**
- Reposting older high-performers is explicitly allowed and encouraged.

## Facebook
- **Primary persona:** Compassionate Contributor (older), Community Builder.
- **Job:** page likes, shares into groups, newsletter subscriptions, website clicks.
- **Formats:** image + link · photo album · native video · Reel.
- **Caption:** **up to 140 words** — Facebook tolerates the longest narrative, but not an essay.
- **Links work** — always include the destination URL (website story preferred over
  homepage; Karimu gets only ~4% of site traffic from social, so make the link
  worth clicking).
- Hashtags: 2–4 maximum, or none.

## LinkedIn
- **Primary persona:** Philanthropic Investor, corporate evangelists, professionals.
- **Job:** impressions, followers, discussion, corporate/ESG relationships.
- **Formats:** text + single image · document/PDF carousel (very strong reach) ·
  native video · article.
- **Copy:** **up to 150 words.** **First 2 lines are the whole game** — lead with the
  number or the counter-intuitive claim, not with "We are excited to share."
- End with a **question that invites discussion**, then the CTA.
- Angles that belong here and nowhere else: cost efficiency (99% to Tanzania vs
  20–35% NGO average), governance, community-led methodology, ESG and employee
  giving, matching-gift programs, Annual Report data, thought leadership on how
  development actually works.
- Tag partner organisations and named individuals when relevant.
- Hashtags: 3–5, professional: `#ESG #SocialImpact #Philanthropy
  #InternationalDevelopment #Tanzania #NonProfit #CorporateGiving`.

## YouTube
- **Job:** subscribers (goal 1,000) and views (goal 2,660).
- **Cadence:** 2/week — 1 Short + 1 long-form.
- **Long-form:** 3–8 min. Hook in first 15s. Community story with data.
  Explicit subscribe ask early and at the end.
- **Shorts:** 15–60s, vertical, burned-in captions, hook in 2s.
- **Titles:** ≤60 characters, concrete and curiosity-driven, no clickbait that the
  video doesn't pay off. Numbers perform.
- **Thumbnails:** **faces, no text** (explicit instruction in the Comms Plan).
  High contrast, one clear subject, brand colors as accent.
- **Description:** 2–3 sentence summary → website link → newsletter subscribe link →
  socials → 3–5 keywords. Chapters for anything over 4 minutes.
- Embed finished videos into website story pages, then share those pages on social.

## WhatsApp (community / broadcast)
- Very short. One fact or one story beat + one photo or a 30s clip.
- No hashtags. A link is fine.
- Roughly one message per week; never more than two.

## Newsletter (support role)
Comms does not write the newsletter (Nelson does) but produces:
- the monthly pre-send subscription post,
- social pull-quotes and cards from newsletter content,
- forwardable assets for the evangelist toolkit.
