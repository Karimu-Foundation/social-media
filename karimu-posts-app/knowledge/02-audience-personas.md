# 02 — Audience & Personas

*Source: Karimu Communications Plan 2025*

## Who social media is actually for

Karimu's **current major donors** are reached one-to-one, by Marianne, Nelson and
Edu — not by Instagram. Social media exists to fill the top of the funnel with the
**three reachable personas** below, and to convert them into newsletter/YouTube/
WhatsApp subscribers.

Write for the reachable personas. Never write a post whose only purpose is to
flatter an existing major donor.

---

## Reachable by social media — write for these

### A. The Compassionate Contributor  *(primary)*
- **Who:** 30–60, all genders, middle-to-high income. Professionals, entrepreneurs,
  retirees with stable finances.
- **Wants:** emotional connection plus tangible results. Moved by stories of change.
  Wants to be part of something larger.
- **Where:** Facebook and LinkedIn (older segment), Instagram (younger segment),
  email.
- **Two high-value sub-groups:**
  - *Professionals* — the bridge into their employers (matching gifts, ESG, ERGs).
  - *Retirees* — highly educated, no longer climbing, seeking meaning and purpose,
    time to give.
- **Content that works:** before/after, a named person's transformation, numbers
  that prove durability, "here is what your money actually bought."

### B. The Global Citizen
- **Who:** Millennials and Gen Z, 20–40, educated, engaged in global issues.
  Students, young professionals.
- **Wants:** transparency, equity, sustainability, clear impact. Community with
  shared values.
- **Where:** Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, X.
- **Note:** low direct donation potential (<$1k/year). **Their value is SHARES.**
  Content for this persona is optimised for shareability and comment volume, not
  conversion.
- **Content that works:** short-form video, myth-busting, "how this actually
  works" explainers, overhead/transparency facts, visual evidence.

### C. The Adventure Philanthropist
- **Who:** 25–45, disposable income for travel and experiences. Professionals,
  digital nomads, people on sabbatical.
- **Wants:** meaningful travel — volunteer trips, cultural exchange, direct contact
  with the community.
- **Where:** Instagram, YouTube, travel blogs, Meetup.
- **Goal:** get them on a volunteer trip or expedition (safari, Kilimanjaro), then
  turn them into an evangelist.
- **Content that works:** volunteer testimonials, trip footage, "what a day on a
  Karimu trip looks like", cultural content.

## Reached individually — do NOT target with feed posts, but keep in mind

| Persona | Note for comms |
|---|---|
| **The Philanthropic Investor** (40+, HNW, $30k–$100k/yr, foundations & family offices) | Skeptical, wants long-term evidence. LinkedIn thought-leadership and detailed reports serve them. Never a hard-sell post. |
| **Wealthy mid-age women, 50+** (USA, Switzerland) | Under-represented in the donor base — a deliberate gap. Feature women leaders, women's stories, maternal health. |
| **Evangelists inside companies** (30–50, USA/Switzerland) | The single highest-leverage target. They need a *shareable toolkit*: forwardable newsletter, annual report, short video. Design content they can forward without embarrassment. |
| **The Community Builder** (25–50, Rotary/Lions, educators, health workers) | 2026 target. Facebook groups, community forums, local events. |

**Geography priority:** USA (tax credit + giving culture) › Switzerland › UK &
Canada › Brazil.

## Psychological motivations to write toward
Legacy · meaning · showing their children they give back · ethical, non-paternalistic
giving · being consulted, not just asked · reinventing themselves · corporate ESG
and employer branding.

## Trust mechanics (the reason numbers matter)
> "Donors like to hear stories with numbers. People that donate the most have some
> personal connection with us — they trust someone here. People will trust Karimu
> if they trust us as individuals."

Implication for content: **put faces and names of Karimu people in the feed** —
Tanzanian staff, volunteers, leaders. Trust transfers person-to-person, not
logo-to-person.
