## Notes / verify

- **Announcement card** — the $30K Tsaayo match is recorded in the source copy; the
  build's start date is not. Do not add a timeline.
- **Quote card** — Lucy Justin Mhando's quote is verbatim from Karimu's own
  published post. Confirm her current title before republishing.
- **News card** — the Tanzanian press quote is paraphrased from the coverage in the
  Super Comms Spreadsheet. **Get the exact wording and the outlet name before
  publishing**, and confirm whether the Minister should be named (political figures
  are a guardrail escalation — he is deliberately unnamed here).
- **Reel cover** — an earlier draft read "$180 buy a farmer", which reads as buying
  a person. Rewritten to "How far does $180 go". Watch for this class of phrasing
  in any cost-per-outcome copy.
- **Savings carousel** — the Federation figures (133.7% savings, 267% profit) were
  left out to keep the data card to three rows. They are available for a second post.
