# 04 — Voice & Tone

*Derived from the published copy in the Super Comms Spreadsheet, plus the
positioning rules in the Comms Plan 2025.*

## Voice attributes

| Attribute | Means | Sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm** | Human, grateful, never corporate | "Thank you! Asante Sana!" |
| **Direct** | Short lines, one idea per line, no throat-clearing | "Water sustains life. Everyone is celebrating." |
| **Evidence-led** | A number in almost every post | "Over 35,000 people now have access to clean water — within 500 meters of their homes." |
| **Dignified** | Community as agent, never object | "All schools now run without Karimu's involvement." |
| **Hopeful, not naive** | Names the hard reality, then the path | "$0.50 a day is where they are starting. It is not where they will end." |
| **Credit-giving** | The donor and the community are the heroes | "The stories of poverty elimination exist because of you." |

## The house sentence shapes

These recur across Karimu's best-performing published copy. Reuse them.

1. **Question hook → hard number → turn.**
   > *"How does a family of five survive on just $0.50 a day? … The findings are
   > sobering: median family income just $0.50/day … But these numbers are not the
   > end of the story. They are the starting point."*

2. **Fragment stack.** Three to six short lines, no connectors.
   > *"Dreams are realized! / Better sanitation that provides privacy / Better
   > classrooms / Better exam scores / Brighter future!"*

3. **Before → After.**
   > *"What was once a two-room dispensary without running water now serves over
   > 40,000 people."*

4. **Named protagonist.**
   > *"Meet Steven Naaman, Head of Ayalagaya High School…"*

5. **"What does Karimu mean to ___?"** — a recurring series opener.

6. **Sustainability proof.** "Today this training is delivered across all wards
   without Karimu's direct involvement."

7. **Closing gratitude.** "Thank you for your support." / "Asante Sana!"

## Length discipline  ⚠ HARD LIMITS

*Added after review, 3 Sep 2026 (Eduardo Lins): "é um bom começo, mas acho muito
texto, tem que pedir pra ele resumir um pouco."*

**Karimu's audience skims.** The default failure of this agent is writing too much.
Draft, then cut by a third. These are ceilings, not targets.

| Element | Ceiling |
|---|---|
| Instagram caption | **90 words** (aim 60–80) |
| Facebook caption | **140 words** |
| LinkedIn post | **150 words** |
| WhatsApp message | **40 words** |
| Slide headline | **8 words** |
| Slide body paragraph | **30 words** |
| Icon bullet | **14 words** |
| Icon bullets per slide | **3** |
| Script (Caveat) payoff line | **10 words** |
| Sticky-box total | **20 words** |

**One text block per slide.** A slide gets a body paragraph *or* a bullet list —
never both. If you need both, it is two slides.

**Cut these first:** explanatory clauses after a number · the second adjective ·
"we are proud to" / "we are excited to" · any sentence restating the previous one ·
setup before the hook · the recap at the end of a caption.

## Word list

**Use:** community-led · dignity · partnership · transformation · from subsistence
to thriving · opportunity · investment · sustainable · self-sustaining · graduates ·
families · without Karimu's involvement · elimination of extreme poverty

**Avoid:** save / saving · rescue · help*less* · needy · the poor · underprivileged ·
third world · giving a voice to · beneficiaries (say *participants*, *families*,
*graduates*, *students*) · empower (overused — show the mechanism instead) ·
"changing lives" as a standalone claim without a number · poverty porn adjectives
(*heartbreaking*, *desperate*, *tragic*) as the frame of an entire post

**Careful with:** "helping" — acceptable in a concrete sentence
("helping farmers expand their practices"), never as the frame of the relationship.

## Tone by channel

| Channel | Register |
|---|---|
| Instagram | Warm, visual-first, short lines, 1–3 emoji max, question hooks |
| Facebook | Same story, slightly longer, more explanatory, links allowed |
| LinkedIn | Professional, data-forward, first sentence must be a hook (feed truncates ~2 lines), no emoji spam, invite discussion |
| YouTube | Spoken-word rhythm, direct address ("you"), explicit subscribe ask |
| WhatsApp | Very short, one fact + one image, conversational |
| Newsletter support post | "Our newsletter goes out on ___. Here's what's inside." |

## Emoji policy
Instagram and Facebook: up to three, meaningful, at line starts or ends
(✨ 🌱 📈 💧 🩺 ❤️ 🤔 👇). LinkedIn: at most one, or none. YouTube titles: none.
Never emoji inside a statistic.

## Language
US English, everywhere. Swahili words used deliberately and translated on first
use in a post: *Asante Sana* (thank you very much), *Karimu* (generous).
