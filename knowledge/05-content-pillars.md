# 05 — Content Pillars & Formats

*Source: Comms Plan 2025 ("Most of our posts followed these series of topics") and
the published copy in the Super Comms Spreadsheet.*

Every post belongs to exactly one pillar. If it belongs to none, it probably
should not be published.

---

## ⭐ Priority formats

*Confirmed by review, 3 Sep 2026 (Eduardo Lins): "esse tipo de post Before/After é
ótimo" · "os posts com dados também são ótimos mostrando impacto."*

Two formats are proven with this audience. **When a story can be told either way,
tell it these ways.** Reach for them before anything else.

### ⭐ A. Before / After
Two images, minimal text, one figure. The transformation carries the post — the
copy must not compete with it.
- **Slide shape:** tab · headline ≤8 words · large BEFORE/AFTER pair · one line of
  result underneath. **No bullet list on this slide.**
- **Works for:** buildings, clinics, kitchens, bathrooms, classrooms, water points,
  a farmer's plot, a school's exam results.
- **Rule:** both photos must be the same subject from a comparable angle, and both
  must be correctly ward-attributed.

### ⭐ B. Data / Impact card
Two or three big numbers, short labels, one script payoff. No paragraphs.
- **Slide shape:** tab · 2–3 stat rows (numeral + ≤6-word label) · script line ·
  CTA. **No body paragraph.**
- **Works for:** program results, sustainability proof, efficiency, reach, cost-per-
  outcome, year-in-numbers.
- **Rule:** every figure states its cohort, ward and year (see `07-impact-facts.md`).

A strong carousel is often exactly: **hook → Before/After → Data card.** Three
slides, very little text.

**Not everything is a carousel.** Announcement cards, quote cards, single-stat
squares, news cards, CTA cards and Reels are all in regular use in the feed. See
`templates/post-formats.md` for the full catalogue and the decision table, and
`examples/format-gallery/` for a rendered example of each.

## 1. Karimu Impact Stories  *(the backbone — ~40% of the calendar)*
A concrete outcome in one ward, told through one person or one place, proven with
numbers.

**Format:**
```
Hook (question or vivid fact)
The before
What the community + Karimu did
The number that proves it
Sustainability line (it runs without us)
Gratitude + CTA
```
**Funnel stage:** Trust · **Best on:** all channels · **Needs:** 1 named person or
place, 1 verified figure, before/after visual if possible.

---

## 2. Karimu Talks
Interview or first-person voice from a Karimu person — Tanzanian staff lead,
program lead, school head, board member. Usually video, often a series.

**Format:** short intro card → who they are and why they matter → the question →
their answer in their own words → what it means → subscribe CTA.
**Funnel stage:** Trust · **Best on:** YouTube (long), IG/FB Reels (clip), LinkedIn.
**Rule:** always name and title the person on screen and in the caption.

---

## 3. Karimu Transformed Volunteers
The volunteer's own change. "In doing so, we ourselves have been changed for the
better." Aimed squarely at the Adventure Philanthropist and at recruiting the next
volunteer cohort.

**Format:** volunteer's name + role → what they expected → the moment that changed
them → what they built → invitation to join.
**Funnel stage:** Attract / Act · **Best on:** Instagram, YouTube, Facebook.
**CTA:** "Learn more about our volunteer program" / contact for the trip.

---

## 4. Shorts / Reels
Short-form video reinforcing a story already published, or a single fact. 15–60s.
**Open with:** "Comment and share! We need your engagement to keep fighting
extreme poverty!" or a hard question.
**Funnel stage:** Attract · **Best on:** YouTube Shorts, IG Reels, FB Reels.
**Rule:** vertical 9:16, burned-in captions, hook in the first 2 seconds.

---

## 5. Newsletter & Channel Subscription Push  *(1× per month, before the send)*
The only post whose sole job is subscription.
**Format:** tease the specific content of the coming newsletter → one image →
one link/CTA. Also used for YouTube subscribe pushes.
**Funnel stage:** Act.

---

## 6. Institutional & Special Dates
Special dates and organisational updates. Recurring dates already in Karimu's
calendar:

| Date | Angle |
|---|---|
| Jan 24 — World Education Day | Teachers and students in Karimu-built schools |
| Mar 8 — International Women's Day | Girls' education, women leaders, maternal health |
| Mothers' Day (May) | Maternal education and health numbers |
| Fathers' Day (June) | Strong families, community approach |
| Nov 19 — World Toilet Day | Sanitation, girls' bathrooms, dignity |
| GivingTuesday (Dec) | "Give in a way that actually has impact" |
| Year-end (Dec) | Year in pictures / year in numbers video |
| New Year (Jan) | Progress letter from leadership |

Also: new staff announcements, new Annual Report, news coverage, government
partnerships, transparency seals (Candid Platinum).

---

## 7. Fundraising Support  *(on request only — never the default)*
Campaign amplification when Fundraising asks. Only pillar allowed to use a
donate CTA. Always paired with a matching-grant or concrete-cost frame
("$290 trains an entrepreneur").

---

## Program themes to rotate across pillars
Education · Health & maternal care · Water & sanitation · Income generation
(entrepreneurship, savings groups, federation, chickens, vanilla, green beans,
biochar) · Girls & women · Community leadership & governance · Transparency &
efficiency.

## Suggested weekly mix (3 posts/week)

| Slot | Pillar |
|---|---|
| Post 1 | Impact Story |
| Post 2 | Rotating: Karimu Talks / Transformed Volunteers / Institutional |
| Video short | Reinforces post 1 or 2 |
| Once a month | Replace post 2 with the Newsletter Subscription push |
