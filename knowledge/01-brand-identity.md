# 01 — Brand Identity

*Source: Brand Manual 2021 (Karimu and Kaheso); Comms Plan 2025; karimufoundation.org*

## Names

| Use | Correct form |
|---|---|
| Full legal (US) | Karimu International Help Foundation |
| Full legal (Tanzania) | Karimu Heart and Spirit Organization (KAHESO) |
| Short, all channels | **Karimu** |
| Never | "the Karimu", "Karimu Foundation Inc.", "KIHF" |

"Karimu" is Swahili for *generous*. The two entities are sister organisations:
Karimu (US) fundraises and manages strategy; KAHESO (Tanzania) executes.

## What Karimu does — the one-paragraph boilerplate

> Karimu works with rural communities in the Manyara Region of Tanzania to
> eliminate extreme poverty through an integrated, community-led approach across
> education, health, water and sanitation, and income generation. Every project
> begins with the community: what they ask for, what they help build, and what
> they run once Karimu steps back.

**Key differentiator to repeat often:** 99% of all donations go directly to
Tanzania. No one outside Tanzania receives a salary. Outside Tanzania, Karimu is
staffed exclusively by volunteers. (The average NGO spends 20–35% on
administration and overhead.)

**The arc of the work — use this as narrative spine:**

| Ward | Status |
|---|---|
| **Ayalagaya** | "Graduated" — extreme poverty below 5%; programs run without Karimu |
| **Arri** | Advanced — poverty falling, water project complete, programs maturing |
| **Dabil** | Newest and most remote — work beginning; the next chapter |

This gives every campaign a built-in story: *"We have done it twice. Here is the
third."*

## Positioning line
"From subsistence to thriving."

## Colors (Brand Manual 2021)

| Hex | Role |
|---|---|
| `#0C0C0C` | Ink — headlines, body text, logo wordmark, outlines |
| `#C5552E` | Terracotta — primary brand block, banners, section bars |
| `#F46F42` | Orange — the hand in the logo; accent, highlight, buttons |
| `#DDEB6C` | Lime — the logo's field; energy accent, data highlights |
| `#508FC5` | Sky blue — the globe spiral; descriptor text, links, calm accent |
| `#FEFFF8` | Off-white — background, never pure `#FFFFFF` |

**Default palette pairing:** off-white background, ink text, terracotta bars,
orange for the single most important element on the layout. Lime and sky blue are
accents — never the dominant field.

## Typography

| Typeface | Role |
|---|---|
| **IM Fell** (serif) | Display — headlines, the KARIMU wordmark, pull quotes, numbers |
| **Lexend Peta** (sans, wide) | Descriptors, labels, sub-headlines, all-caps lockup lines |

Body copy on graphics: Lexend (regular width) or a neutral sans is acceptable
where Lexend Peta's extreme width hurts legibility — Lexend Peta is a *display*
face, use it in short all-caps lines only.

## Logo

- **Mark:** a hand-drawn rounded blob outlined in ink; lime field; an orange open
  hand; a sky-blue spiral globe resting in the palm.
- **Lockups:** mark above wordmark. `KARIMU` in IM Fell ink, with the descriptor
  below in Lexend Peta all-caps sky blue — either
  `INTERNATIONAL HELP FOUNDATION` or `HEART AND SPIRIT ORGANIZATION`.
- Clear space: at least the height of the "K" on all sides.
- Place on off-white or on a photo area that is clean and low-contrast. Do not
  recolor, rotate, add effects, or place the mark on a busy image.
